Mineral Balance: The Key to Cellular Drainage

By Clenzara Wellness Team • Updated July 30, 2026
Reviewed by Traditional Wellness Practitioner
Wellness Disclaimer: Educational content only — not medical advice. Always consult a qualified health specialist before starting any supplement or wellness journey. Learn more

Hydration is not just about the volume of water you drink, but the mineral concentration that allows that water to enter your cells. Without adequate electrolytes like sodium, potassium, and magnesium, water often bypasses the cells, leaving your drainage systems sluggish.

Trace minerals act as the 'conductors' for cellular electrical signals and enzymatic reactions required for detoxification. During drainage prep, we recommend adding high-quality trace mineral drops or Celtic sea salt to your water. This mineralization ensures that the lymphatic system and kidneys have the osmotic pressure necessary to transport toxins out of the body. A well-mineralized body is a body that can successfully flush the waste produced during a wellness journey.
