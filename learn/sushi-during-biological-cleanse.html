Can I eat sushi during a wellness journey?

By Clenzara Wellness Team • Updated July 30, 2026
Reviewed by Traditional Wellness Practitioner
Educational content only — not medical advice. Always consult a qualified health specialist before starting any wellness journey.

No, it is generally recommended to avoid sushi and raw fish during a wellness journey. Raw or undercooked seafood can carry larvae and biological matter that may re-infect your gut, counteracting the progress of your cleanse. Focus on high-quality, cooked proteins to prevent additional biological exposure while your body heals.

While sushi is a favorite "Clean Girl" staple, the risk of introducing new organisms during a wellness journey is high. Most traditional wellness journeys (like the Clenzara 15-herb blend) aim to eliminate existing infections. Adding raw fish into the mix creates a revolving door effect. To maximize results, stick to steamed white fish, grilled chicken, or plant-based proteins until your gut reset is complete and your digestive fire is fully restored.
