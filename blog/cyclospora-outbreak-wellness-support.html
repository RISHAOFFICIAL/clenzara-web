The current foodborne concern enters the body through contaminated fresh produce and can challenge the intestinal environment. Common responses to this digestive disruption include bloating, abdominal discomfort, loose stools, fatigue, and nausea. These indicators can appear within 1 to 2 weeks of exposure.

While most healthy individuals find their rhythm again within days to weeks, the experience can disrupt gut balance — particularly the intestinal mucus barrier and the delicate microbiome terrain. Staying Protected: Prevention for the Future

To support your biological harmony and reduce the risk of future digestive disruptions:

  • Wash Produce Thoroughly: Always wash basil, cilantro, and other leafy greens under cold running water. Consider a Traditional soak with apple cider vinegar.
  • Know Your Source: Monitor FDA and local health department updates for recall expansions in your area.
  • Maintain Drainage: Ensure your body's elimination pathways (liver, lymph, colon) are open and active before introducing deep cleansing protocols.
  • Kitchen Hygiene: Use separate cutting boards for fresh produce and raw proteins to prevent cross-contamination.
