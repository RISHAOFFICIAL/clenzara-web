The Drainage Funnel Framework

Think of your body's elimination systems as a funnel. The colon is at the bottom, followed by the liver, kidneys, lymph, and finally the skin and lungs at the top. If the bottom of the funnel (the colon) is plugged, everything above it backs up. Achieving **Biological Harmony** requires a bottom-up approach to drainage support.
