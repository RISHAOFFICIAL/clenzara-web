If you've been spending any time in wellness circles recently, you've likely heard the term **Intracellular Cleansing**. It's often presented as the "holy grail" of biological restoration—a way to reclaim your electric vitality and eliminate the mucus that anchors stagnation in your system.

But there is a beginner's mistake that is so common it's almost a rite of passage: Attempting to clear the gut before opening the exit doors.

In traditional wellness, we understand that you cannot "clean" a room if the trash cans are full and the front door is locked. Your body's exit doors are your **intracellular drainage pathways**. When these pathways are closed, any biological matter you attempt to harmonize simply recirculates, leading to the dreaded "detox flux."

In this guide, we'll break down how to start opening drainage pathways naturally in a simple, 4-day onboarding phase.

The Drainage Funnel: Why Order Matters

Think of your body as a funnel. At the narrow bottom is your **Colon** (elimination). Above that is your **Liver** and **Bile**. Above that are your **Kidneys** and **Lymphatic System**. At the very top are your **Cells**.

If the bottom of the funnel (your colon) is blocked, everything above it gets backed up. Most beginners try to start at the top—clearing the cells—which only creates a toxic logjam. **Biological Restoration** requires working from the bottom up.

Step 1: Focus on Mucus Elimination (Days 1–2)

Your colon is the primary exit route for waste. If you aren't having 1–3 daily bowel movements, your drainage funnel is closed. For the first two days, your entire focus should be on "moving the waste."
