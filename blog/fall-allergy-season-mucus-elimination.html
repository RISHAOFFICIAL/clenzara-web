If your drainage pathways are sluggish, this "seasonal shift" causes the mucus to become more apparent as the body tries to vent it through the respiratory tract.

By focusing on Intracellular Cleansing, we can reduce this load before the peak of the season, allowing the body to process environmental triggers without the dramatic inflammatory response.
