The Timeline of Digestive Disruption

Understanding the stages of a foodborne challenge allows you to match your Traditional Wellness support to your body's current needs. In the case of Cyclospora and similar concerns, we typically see a three-stage timeline:

Stage 1: The Incubation Window (Days 2–11)

After encountering unwanted biological matter through fresh produce, there is a quiet period. Your system is attempting to maintain balance, but the disruption is beginning to take root. Most people do not notice anything during this stage, but the "terrain" of the gut is already shifting.

Stage 2: The Active Disruption (Weeks 1–4)

This is when the body signals its disharmony. Watery stools, abdominal heaviness, and significant bloating are common. Without support, this stage can fluctuate, with symptoms seeming to improve for a few days before returning. This "relapsing" pattern is a hallmark of certain environmental stressors.

Stage 3: The Restoration Phase (Weeks 4–6+)

Even after the acute signs have passed, the intestinal landscape often remains depleted. Lingering fatigue and "brain fog" can persist as the body works to rebuild its alkaline foundation and restore its natural guardians.
