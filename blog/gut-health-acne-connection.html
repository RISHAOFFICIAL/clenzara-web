The Elimination Organ Hierarchy

Your body has a specific order of operations for removing waste. The hierarchy is simple: Colon > Liver > Kidneys > Lymph > Skin. Under ideal conditions, metabolic byproducts and biological waste exit through the colon and kidneys.

However, when the colon is stagnant and **The Sentinel** (your gut barrier) is compromised, the liver becomes overburdened. When the liver can no longer filter the load, the body shifts the burden to the skin. Acne and breakouts are often simply "venting" mechanisms for internal stagnation. By opening the primary drainage pathways, you take the pressure off your skin, allowing it to return to its natural, clear state.

Intracellular Cleansing for a Clear Complexion

One of the primary drivers of skin disharmony is the presence of **biofilms** in the gut. These protective layers of mucus shield unwanted biological matter, which release irritating metabolic byproducts into your system. These irritants circulate through the blood and lymph, eventually manifesting as inflammation on the skin.

**Intracellular Cleansing** focuses on dissolving these biofilms and clearing the "intracellular space" of these irritants. This is the difference between a temporary "clear" and a permanent state of **Biological Harmony**.
